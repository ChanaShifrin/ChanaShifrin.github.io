Late deliveries are quietly costing the marketplace its repeat customers

Analyzed 100K orders across 9 tables. Customers whose first order arrived over a week late rarely ordered again.

  • PostgreSQL
  • Cohort analysis

Question: Do late deliveries hurt repeat purchases?

Approach: Joined 9 tables, built signup cohorts, and compared retention by delay bucket with window functions.

Result: Orders 7+ days late averaged 2.1 stars vs. 4.3 on time.

Recommendation: Add a delivery-promise buffer in the three worst regions.

Leads with revenue vs. target, margin, and top-losing product lines. Surfaced a discount pattern eroding margin in one region.

Approach: Defined three KPIs with the sales team, then built filters by region and rep.

Did the new checkout button actually lift conversions?

Checked for sample-ratio problems, then ran a two-proportion test. Lift was concentrated on mobile, so I recommended a staged rollout.
